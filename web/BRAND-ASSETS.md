# Admiral brand asset

`admiral-logo.svg` is an unchanged copy of the official light-on-dark Admiral
logo served by the navigation bar at [admrl.co](https://admrl.co/).

- Source: https://admrl.co/_app/immutable/assets/admiral-light.DiddamFK.svg
- Retrieved: 7 October 2026
- The logo and Admiral name remain the property of Admiral Operations Pty Ltd.
  The official website states “All rights reserved”; this note does not grant
  a new licence to the brand asset.

Dashboard brand colours follow the same official site's stylesheet:
https://admrl.co/_app/immutable/assets/0.BrrgXVar.css
(`--color-blue: #2156e9`, `--color-blue-soft: #8aa7f5`, and technical-mode
`--color-accent: #2de282`). The SVG's original blue fill is `#2056EA`.

The logo is bundled locally so the on-device dashboard needs no external
asset connection.
